Sardine Pasta

This sardine pasta, with fresh and sun-dried tomatoes, artichokes, and hints of lemon and garlic, is a light yet flavorful dish that’s made for summer dinners on the porch.

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package spaghetti noodles
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 yellow onion, chopped
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh garlic
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 1/2 cup olives
  • 4 marinated artichoke hearts
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced, divided
  • 2 cans sardines, preferably boneless
  • 1 tablespoon Greek all-purpose seasoning
  • 1 cup fresh spinach
  • 2 tablespoons sun-dried tomatoes
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh thyme or basil

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil; add spaghetti and cook until tender with a bite, about 10 minutes. Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain spaghetti and keep warm.
  2. Meanwhile, heat a cast iron skillet with olive oil over medium heat. Add onions, garlic, and tomatoes. Stir and cook until tomatoes begin to burst, about 3 minutes.
  3. Add olives and artichokes. Stir and cook for 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in lemon juice.
  4. Add sardines and another tablespoon olive oil, if needed. Stir sardines gently into the sauce, just until well combined.
  5. Add Greek seasoning, pasta water, and spinach to the sardine mixture and mix until combined. Add in pasta a little at a time and toss well. Add sun-dried tomatoes and fresh thyme; remove from heat.
  6. Once plated, drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with lemon zest, and serve.
